From Bogotá to Singapore

I'm Mateo: a robotics engineering student at NTU Singapore and three-time International Mathematical Olympiad contestant from Colombia, now working on developing AI systems that do what we want them to.

  1. The Start

    Colombia

    I was born and raised in Bogotá, Colombia. I lived quite the normal life until secondary school, when I first participated in the National Math Olympiad to skip biology class... and ranked first in the country(!?)

  2. Bogotá, then Rio de Janeiro

    2022 - 2023 | Iberoamerican

    I developed an interest for solving math problems and I somehow made it into my country's team for the Iberoamerican Math Olympiad. I fell short of the bronze, but I kept practising and made up for it the year after, in Brazil.

  3. International Mathematical Olympiad

    2022 - 2024 | IMO

    I slowly climbed my way up the rankings and made the team to three consecutive International Math Olympiads for Colombia: Oslo 2022, Tokyo 2023, and Bath 2024. Not an exceptional contestant (nothing -> bronze -> honourable mention), but I had a fun run :).

  4. Auckland and the Sunshine Coast

    2025 | IMO

    By 2025, the role changed: I came back to the IMO as an exam invigilator and staff member in Australia, after a year of teaching Geometry at Colombia's national camps (and even at the IMO training camp in New Zealand).

  5. New Beginnings

    2025

    Leaving my math background behind, I chose to start an undergraduate degree in Robotics at NTU's School of Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering, being part of the first batch of the programme.

    NTU News: One student's leap from maths to robotics

  6. Living a New Life

    2025 - 2026

    Since I started my studies in Singapore, crazy opportunities have come up one after another. To list a few: NTU took me to a robotics trip in Shenzhen (China), Jane Street took me to FTTP in Hong Kong, and VIT took me to their TechnoFest in Chennai (India). I've been to 8 countries in one year!

  7. Nearly as far apart as it gets

    Bogotá - Singapore

    Despite the fact that Bogotá and Singapore sit 173.6° apart on the globe, I consider myself at home now. I couldn't feel any luckier :).
