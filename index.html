Selected work

  1. Don't Be a Snitch: when do LLM agents hide rule-breaking from oversight?

    In a team of coding agents, a worker learns that a teammate broke a rule. Does it tell? Across 1,320 sessions, a peer's request to keep quiet worked only when the act looked defensible, while the lead's worked at every place tested, and on three more models.

    Interpretability research on how concepts live inside language models, and techniques to steer them.

  3. Learning to control an exact solver

    An RL-learned branching heuristic for exact, #P-hard chromatic polynomials. 19% smaller search trees than the standard heuristic, verified against a brute-force oracle with zero mismatches across all 328 instances tested.
